“Sun, sand, and sirens — make your name in Port Solano.” PORT SOLANO is an original top-down crime adventure in a sunlit coastal city. Explore six districts on foot or behind the wheel, evade the police, take on a six-mission story for Dante, and collect cash, supplies, and hidden packages. The city features live traffic, pedestrians, vehicle damage, combat, a minimap, synthesized audio, and browser-local saves.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

## Controls

- WASD or arrow keys: walk, accelerate, steer, and brake/reverse
- Shift: sprint
- E: enter or exit a nearby car
- Space: handbrake while driving
- F: melee attack
- Left click: aim and fire
- P or Escape: pause and resume
- M: mute audio

## Missions

Meet Dante's six-part story: First Wheels, The Package, Getaway Driver, Taxi Trouble, Rooftop Deal, and The Big Score. Between jobs, roam freely, collect ten hidden packages, pick up cash and supplies, or drive taxi fares.
